#### AST 443 / PHY 517 Observational Techniques in Astronomy: Final Lab
## Determining the Age of Open Cluster M67 via BV Photometry and Isochrone Fitting

### Abstract
In this lab, we utilize a ground based 14.5 inch telescope along with a B and V broad-band filter
at Mt. Stony Brook observatory to perform BV photometry on open cluster M67 (NGC 2682). From
these images we measure the brightness and color of several cluster stars and build a color–magnitude
diagram (CMD), a plot of stellar brightness versus color, which we then compare to computer generated
stellar isochrones from the PARSEC stellar evolution library (Bressan et al. (2012)) to predict the
cluster’s age. Through our analysis we were able to successfully identify a lower bound of 1 Gyr but
struggled to differentiate between upper bounds and support the expected literature value of 4 Gyr
(Richer et al. (1998)) due to lack of stars in the main sequence turnoff point.
